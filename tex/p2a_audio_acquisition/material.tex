\section*{Material}

For this session, you will need:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The latest version of STM32CubeIDE and STM32CubeMX installed on your computer (for further information, see INSTALL.md on the course's GitHub) ;
    \item The Nucleo MCU board with the sensing and power-management board and the radio board plugged in ;
    \item A USB cable to connect your computer to the Nucleo.
\end{itemize}
