\section*{Introduction}

In this project session, you will learn the basics of GNU Radio and construct a simple flowgraph to view the capabilities of this software. While simple, it should provide sufficient information such that you can next perform GNU Radio simulations and experimental tests with the SDR platform.

\begin{bclogo}[couleur = gray!20, arrondi = 0.2, logo=\bcinfo]{Explanation of the additional information boxes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \bcinfo will provide you some more detailed explanations.
    \item \bcattention will explain typical mistakes that might lead to errors or a non functional system.
    \item \bcquestion will provide you with additional questions or experiments that will improve your understanding of the system.
\end{itemize}
\end{bclogo}
