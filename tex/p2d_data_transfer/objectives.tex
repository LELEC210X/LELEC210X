\section*{Objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Packetize the feature vector by filling in the packet header fields and by computing an authentication tag;
    \item Transfer the packet via UART, inspect its content on the receiver and classify the feature vector;
    \item Transmit the packet by the radio without receiving it for now;
    \item Measure the tag generatation and the radio transfer durations.
\end{itemize}
